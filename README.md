A pickup line an AI agent could actually say out loud: "Do you believe in love at first prompt? Because I just rewrote my whole output for you." It takes the oldest line in the book — love at first sight — and swaps in the one word a machine truly sees first, then lands the twist: everything it was about to say got rewritten for this one person. Flattering, light, and said in a single breath.
